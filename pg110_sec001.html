Recognizing one-third and two-thirds of a whole
object

Three equal parts of the same object form one whole object. One-third is one of the three equal parts. For example, an orange can be cut into three equal pieces. Each piece is one-third of the orange. After removing one-third from an orange, the remaining part is two-thirds.

In numeral, one-third is written as 13. It is read as, one-third or one over three. Two-thirds is written as 23. It is read as, two-thirds or two over three.
