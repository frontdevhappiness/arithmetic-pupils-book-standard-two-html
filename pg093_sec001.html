Division

Division using objects

Division is the process of distributing objects into groups of equal number of objects. It is the opposite of multiplication. It is also known as repeated subtraction.

The operation of division is represented by the sign ÷.
For example, 8 ÷ 4 = 2 is read as, 8 divided by 4 equals 2.

When dividing objects, it is better to distribute one object at a time, in rounds.

Example

Divide 6 stars into 2 groups. How many stars will each group get?
