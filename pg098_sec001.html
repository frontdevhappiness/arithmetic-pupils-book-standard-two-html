Division by using repeated subtraction

Repeated subtraction is the process of subtracting the same number repeatedly. The process stops when a 0 is obtained.

Example 1

6 ÷ 2 =

Solution

Subtract 2 from 6 repeatedly.

6 − 2 = 4

4 − 2 = 2

2 − 2 = 0

Repeated subtraction has been done 3 times.
Therefore, 6 ÷ 2 = 3
