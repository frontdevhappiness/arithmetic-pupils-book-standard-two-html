Word problems on division

Word problems involving division are questions written
in one or more sentence.

Example 1

A story book has 12 pages. Dezi can read 3 pages of
the book everyday. How long will she take to read
the whole book?

Solution

Divide 12 pages by 3.

12 ÷ 3 = 4

Therefore, Dezi will take 4 days to read the whole
book.

Example 2

A group of 6 pupils shared 36 mangoes equally. How
many mangoes did each pupil get?

Solution

Divide 36 by 6.

36 ÷ 6 = 6

Therefore, each pupil got 6 mangoes.
