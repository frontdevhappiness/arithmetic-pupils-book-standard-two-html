Recognizing a quarter of a whole object

Four equal parts of an object form one whole object. A
quarter is one of the four equal parts. For example, an
orange can be cut into four equal pieces. Each piece is
a quarter of the orange.

In numerals, a quarter is written as 14. It is read as, a
quarter or one over four.
