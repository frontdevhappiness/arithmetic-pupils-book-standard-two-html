Example. Three hundred and fourteen minus two hundred and eight. The numbers are arranged vertically under hundreds, tens, and ones. Solution. Three hundred and fourteen minus two hundred and eight equals one hundred and six. Steps. One. Subtract ones: four minus eight. It is not sufficient. Two. Take one group of tens from one ten. Regroup it into ten ones. Remember zero tens remain. Add ones: ten plus four equals fourteen. Three. Subtract ones: fourteen minus eight equals six. Write six in the ones place. Four. Subtract tens: zero minus zero equals zero. Write zero in the tens place. Five. Subtract hundreds: three minus two equals one. Write one in the hundreds place. Therefore, the answer is one hundred and six.
