Subtracting numbers horizontally without regrouping. Numbers are subtracted starting from the right to the left.Example. 247 minus 123. Solution. Subtract by place value, starting from the right. In the ones place, 7 minus 3 equals 4. In the tens place, 4 minus 2 equals 2. In the hundreds place, 2 minus 1 equals 1. Therefore, 247 minus 123 equals 124.
