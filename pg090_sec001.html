Word problems on multiplication

Word problems involving multiplication are questions written in one or more sentence.

Example 1

There are 6 flower beds in a garden. Every flower bed has 5 seedlings. How many seedlings are there in the garden?

Solution

Number of flower beds = 6.

Number of seedlings in each flower bed = 5.

Multiply the numbers:

6×5=6+6+6+6+6=30.

Therefore, the garden has 30 flower seedlings.

Example 2

One car can carry 22 passengers at once. How many passengers can be carried by 3 cars of the same capacity?

Solution

Number of passengers in one car = 22

Number of cars = 3

Multiply:

2 2

× 3

6 6

Therefore, 3 cars can carry 66 passengers.
