Word problems involving multiplication

Word problems involving multiplication are questions written in one or more sentences.

There are 6 flower beds in a garden. Every flower bed has 5 seedlings. How many seedlings are there in the garden?

Solution

Number of flower beds = 6.
Number of seedlings in each flower bed = 5.
Multiply the numbers: 6 × 5 = 6+6+6+6+6 = 30.

Therefore, the garden has 30 flower seedlings.
